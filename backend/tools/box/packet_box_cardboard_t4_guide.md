# Cutting guide: cardboard box for the current gripper, 4 mm board

Written by `box_pattern.py` from the same numbers as the drawing. All sizes are mm.

The box is one piece. The top of each end wall folds inward as a ledge flap, and a slot just under the fold lets the gripper's hook reach in under it.

## Check the board first

- Thickness: this guide is for 4 mm board. If yours differs by more than 0.5 mm, run the script again with your thickness.
- Board: single-wall C-flute. The blank is 904.0 x 467.9.
- From a 20 x 20 x 34 in wardrobe box: the blank spans two panels, and crease 3 goes on the box's own corner crease. See the drawing ending `_on_wardrobe_box.svg`.
- From a flat 24 x 36 in sheet (609.6 x 914.4): one blank fits with 10 to spare in length, so start from a square end.
- Flutes (the ribs inside the board) run up the walls, across the 904.0 length of the blank.
- Mark out on the plain inside face. That face ends up inside the box, and every fold goes toward you.

## Cut

1. Bottom flaps. Keep four flaps below the walls and cut away the rest:
   side A from 27.5 to 264.5 and side B from 467.5 to 704.5, both from the bottom edge up to 98.5;
   end 1 from 269.5 to 462.5 and end 2 from 709.5 to 901.5, both from 4.0 up to 102.5.
   That leaves a 5.0 wide slot at each of creases 2, 3 and 4.
2. Tab. Keep the strip from the left end to 25.0, between 106.5 and 241.4 up. Cut away the corners above and below it.
3. Wall tops. Side A ends at 249.4 up and side B at 251.4 up. The end walls end at 245.4 up, outside their flaps.
4. Ledge flaps. Above each end wall keep a flap up to 305.4: end 1 from 269.5 to 462.5, end 2 from 709.5 to 901.5.
5. Lid. Above side A keep the lid from 33.7 to 258.3, up to 442.9, and the tuck flap from 88.0 to 204.0, up to the top edge. The lid is shorter than side A on purpose, and the tuck flap is narrow so that it drops between the two ledge flaps.
6. Jaw slots. Cut a slot right out of each end wall: end 1 from 298.0 to 434.0, end 2 from 738.0 to 874.0, between 221.4 and 243.4 up. The top of the slot stops 2.0 below the ledge flap crease. Do not cut into the crease. Round the two top corners a little.

## Crease

Press a blunt edge along a ruler on this face. Do not cut through. Every fold goes toward you.

- Creases 1 to 4: up the walls at 25.0, 267.0, 465.0 and 707.0.
- Bottom flaps: side A and side B at 98.5 up, end 1 and end 2 at 102.5 up.
- Ledge flaps: across each end wall at 245.4 up, right across the top of the jaw slot.
- Lid: across side A at 249.4 up.
- Tuck flap: at 442.9 up.

## Liner (5 mm foil bubble)

Cut with scissors.

| Piece | Size | Number | Goes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Floor | 237.0 x 193.0 | 1 | On the floor |
| Side | 237.0 x 128.9 | 2 | Along each long wall, standing on the floor piece |
| End | 183.0 x 128.9 | 2 | Between the side pieces at each end |
| Top | 213.4 x 193.0 | 1 | Laid on top of the wall pieces, centred, after the food is in |

Cut a notch 146 wide and 22 deep in the middle of the top edge of each end piece. The notch and the short top piece keep the jaw slots clear.

## Fold and fix

1. Fold the four walls into a tube. Glue or tape the tab inside the free edge of end 2.
2. Fold the two end bottom flaps in, then the two side bottom flaps over them. Tape along the seam.
3. Put the floor, side and end liner pieces in, if you use them.
4. Pack the food. Keep the top of each end wall clear for about 14 mm inside.
5. Fold the two ledge flaps in.
6. Close the lid onto the ledge flaps, tuck its flap inside the front wall and tape the whole front edge, corners included. The tape matters: the lid holds the ledge flaps down when the hooks lift.

The tags (218 x 190) fit on the lid, which is 224.6 x 193.5. A sticker bigger than the lid needs its white margin trimmed.
