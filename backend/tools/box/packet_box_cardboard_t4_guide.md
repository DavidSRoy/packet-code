# Cutting guide: cardboard box with hand holes, 4 mm board

Written by `box_pattern.py` from the same numbers as the drawing. All sizes are mm.

The box is one piece. The drone lifts it by a hand hole in each end wall.

## Check the board first

- Thickness: this guide is for 4 mm board. If yours differs by more than 0.5 mm, run the script again with your thickness.
- Flutes (the ribs inside the board) run up the walls, across the 904.0 length of the blank.
- Mark out on the plain inside face. That face ends up inside the box, and every fold goes toward you.
- The drawing ending `_on_wardrobe_box.svg` shows where the blank sits on a 20 x 20 x 34 in wardrobe box. Crease 3 uses one of that box's own corner creases.

## Mark out

Draw a rectangle 904.0 x 467.4. Take every measurement from its left end or its bottom edge.

## Cut

1. Bottom flaps. Keep four flaps below the walls and cut away the rest:
   side A from 27.5 to 264.5 and side B from 467.5 to 704.5, both from the bottom edge up to 98.5;
   end 1 from 269.5 to 462.5 and end 2 from 709.5 to 901.5, both from 4.0 up to 102.5.
   That leaves a 5.0 wide slot at each of creases 2, 3 and 4.
2. Tab. Keep the strip from the left end to 25.0, between 106.5 and 240.9 up. Cut away the corners above and below it.
3. Wall tops. Side B ends at 250.9 up. Cut away everything above it between creases 3 and 4.
4. Dust flaps. Above each end wall keep a flap up to 304.9: end 1 from 269.5 to 462.5, end 2 from 709.5 to 901.5.
5. Lid. Above side A keep the lid from 22.5 to 269.5, up to 442.4, and the tuck flap from 29.0 to 263.0, up to the top edge.
6. Lid and dust flap. They share one cut at 269.5, from 244.9 to 304.9 up. Cut out the small notch beside it, between 267.0 and 269.5 and between 244.9 and 248.9 up, so the lid can close.
7. Hand holes. On each end wall cut the two sides and the bottom of the hole: end 1 from 321.0 to 411.0, end 2 from 761.0 to 851.0, between 195.9 and 225.9 up. Do not cut the top edge.

## Crease

Press a blunt edge along a ruler on this face. Do not cut through. Every fold goes toward you.

- Creases 1 to 4: up the walls at 25.0, 267.0, 465.0 and 707.0.
- Bottom flaps: side A and side B at 98.5 up, end 1 and end 2 at 102.5 up.
- Dust flaps: across each end wall at 244.9 up.
- Lid: across side A at 248.9 up.
- Tuck flap: at 442.4 up.
- Hand hole flaps: the top edge of each hole, at 225.9 up.

## Liner (5 mm foil bubble)

Cut with scissors. Two of each.

| Piece | Size | Goes |
|---|---|---|
| Floor and lid | 237.0 x 193.0 | One on the floor, one glued under the lid |
| Side | 237.0 x 132.4 | Along each long wall, standing on the floor piece |
| End | 183.0 x 132.4 | Between the side pieces at each end |

Cut a notch 110 wide and 51 deep in the middle of the top edge of each end piece. It keeps the hand hole clear for the jaw.

## Fold and fix

1. Fold the four walls into a tube. Glue or tape the tab inside the free edge of end 2.
2. Fold the two end bottom flaps in, then the two side bottom flaps over them. Tape along the seam.
3. Put the liner in, if you use it.
4. Pack the food. Keep the top of each end wall clear for about 13 mm inside.
5. Fold the two dust flaps in.
6. Close the lid, tuck its flap inside the front wall and tape the front edge.

Leave the hand hole flaps flat. The drone's jaws push them in.
