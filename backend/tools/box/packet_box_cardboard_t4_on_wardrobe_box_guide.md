# Layout guide: the blank on a 20 x 20 x 34 in wardrobe box, 4 mm board

Written by `box_pattern.py` from the same numbers as the drawings. All sizes are mm.

Every distance here is measured from two lines the wardrobe box already has: the corner crease in the middle of the opened box, and its bottom edge. The drawing ending `_on_wardrobe_box.svg` shows where the blank sits.

## Prepare the wardrobe box

1. Measure the board thickness. This guide is for 4 mm board. If yours differs by more than 0.5 mm, run the script again with your thickness. The pattern does not work with board of 6 mm or more.
2. Find the corner where the box is glued together. Cut the box open down that corner.
3. Cut the top flaps and the bottom flaps off along their creases. That leaves a flat sheet of four panels with three creases, about 2032 x 864.
4. Lay the sheet with the plain inside face up. Hand holes and bar notches are near one end: put that end away from you. The edge toward you is the bottom edge.
5. The middle crease is the corner crease. Crease 3 of the blank goes on it, and it already folds the right way.
6. Check the size. Each panel beside the corner crease must be at least 470 wide (the listing says 508) and 508 tall (the listing says 864).
7. Check that no hand hole, notch or extra crease falls inside the blank, which reaches 507.9 up from the bottom edge. If one does, add the same amount to every "up" distance to move the blank clear. There is 356 to spare.

The flutes (the ribs inside the board) run away from you, up the walls of the blank. Every fold goes toward you.

## Cut

1. Bottom flaps. Keep four flaps below the walls and cut away the rest:
   side A from 437.5 left to 200.5 left and side B from 2.5 right to 239.5 right, both from 40.0 up to 138.5;
   end 1 from 195.5 left to 2.5 left and end 2 from 244.5 right to 436.5 right, both from 44.0 up to 142.5.
   That leaves a 5.0 wide slot at each of creases 2, 3 and 4.
2. Tab. Keep the strip from 465.0 left to 440.0 left, between 146.5 and 281.4 up. Cut away the corners above and below it.
3. Wall tops. Side A ends at 289.4 up and side B at 291.4 up. The end walls end at 285.4 up, outside their flaps.
4. Ledge flaps. Above each end wall keep a flap up to 345.4: end 1 from 195.5 left to 2.5 left, end 2 from 244.5 right to 436.5 right.
5. Lid. Above side A keep the lid from 431.3 left to 206.7 left, up to 482.9, and the tuck flap from 377.0 left to 261.0 left, up to 507.9. The lid is shorter than side A on purpose, and the tuck flap is narrow so that it drops between the two ledge flaps.
6. Jaw slots. Cut a slot right out of each end wall: end 1 from 167.0 left to 31.0 left, end 2 from 273.0 right to 409.0 right, between 261.4 and 283.4 up. The top of the slot stops 2.0 below the ledge flap crease. Do not cut into the crease. Round the two top corners a little.

## Crease

Press a blunt edge along a ruler on this face. Do not cut through. Every fold goes toward you.

- Creases 1 to 4: up the walls at 440.0 left, 198.0 left, 0 and 242.0 right.
- Crease 3 is the corner crease the wardrobe box already has. Do not score it again.
- Bottom flaps: side A and side B at 138.5 up, end 1 and end 2 at 142.5 up.
- Ledge flaps: across each end wall at 285.4 up, right across the top of the jaw slot.
- Lid: across side A at 289.4 up.
- Tuck flap: at 482.9 up.

## Liner (5 mm foil bubble)

Cut with scissors.

| Piece | Size | Number | Goes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Floor | 237.0 x 193.0 | 1 | On the floor |
| Side | 237.0 x 128.9 | 2 | Along each long wall, standing on the floor piece |
| End | 183.0 x 128.9 | 2 | Between the side pieces at each end |
| Top | 223.0 x 193.0 | 1 | Laid on top of the wall pieces, centred, after the food is in |

Cut a notch 146 wide and 22 deep in the middle of the top edge of each end piece. The notch and the short top piece keep the jaw slots clear.

## Fold and fix

1. Fold the four walls into a tube. Glue or tape the tab inside the free edge of end 2.
2. Fold the two end bottom flaps in, then the two side bottom flaps over them. Tape along the seam.
3. Put the floor, side and end liner pieces in, if you use them.
4. Pack the food. Keep the top of each end wall clear for about 9 mm inside.
5. Fold the two ledge flaps in.
6. Close the lid onto the ledge flaps, tuck its flap inside the front wall and tape the whole front edge, corners included. The tape matters: the lid holds the ledge flaps down when the jaws lift.

The tags (218 x 190) fit on the lid, which is 224.6 x 193.5. A sticker bigger than the lid needs its white margin trimmed.
