Starts 3 m up, off to the side and turned, with some wobble, and closes in on the way down, like the misaligned descents in the Python sim.

Geometry: Master Assembly v60 (gripper jaws RP-019 v16 as boxes, drive housing, battery plate, landing legs), box BX-005 v12 and lid BX-006 v7 with the 218 × 200 mm sticker. The camera can sit anywhere you like here; check that a real bracket can hold it there. Readability here is a quick estimate (whole tag in view, not blocked, at least 2.7 px per cell, blur). tools/sim/lid_camera_sim.py runs the real detector and gives the scored numbers.
